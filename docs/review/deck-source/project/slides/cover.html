Paya · Fall 2026

Speaker A (Rufayl). At a shop counter, the cashier often looks at the customer's phone and decides the payment worked. Paya fixes that: the till only says PAID when the money has really moved and our ledger has recorded it. Everything you will see is made up: no real people, no real money, and the Raast network is simulated. Introduce the team: Taha (payments and ledger), Harris (reconciliation and AI), Kanz (security and identity checks), Mudaser (reliability and Mission Control).
