Journey: a shop QR payment, with refunds if needed

Speaker A (Rufayl). Raast shop payments grew from about 4.9 million to about 180 million in one year (SBP Governor's Annual Reports, page 58, checked by us). But three things are still hard. One: a screenshot is not proof of payment. Two: shops match payments to orders by hand. Three: networks fail, and a lost reply is where money goes wrong. Our journey is the brief's shop payment, including the refund path, followed from the phone all the way to Finance.
